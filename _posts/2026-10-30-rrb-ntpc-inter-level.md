# Railway Recruitment Board (RRB)
## RRB NTPC Under Graduate Level (10+2) Recruitment CEN 07/2026
### Short Details of Notification

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the notification for Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC) Under Graduate / 10+2 Level Advt No. 07/2026. Interested candidates who meet the eligibility criteria can apply online. Please read the full notification before applying.

### Important Dates
*   **Application Begin:** 25/10/2026
*   **Last Date for Apply Online:** 13/11/2026
*   **Pay Exam Fee Last Date:** 13/11/2026
*   **Correction Date:** 15-20 November 2026
*   **Exam Date (CBT 1):** To be Notified

### Application Fee
*   **General / OBC / EWS:** ₹ 500/-
*   **SC / ST / PH:** ₹ 250/-
*   **Female All Category:** ₹ 250/-
*   **Note:** ₹ 400/- will be refunded for UR/OBC/EWS and ₹ 250/- for SC/ST/Female after appearing in the Stage I Exam. Pay the fee through Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking or UPI.

### Age Limit as on 01/01/2027
*   **Minimum Age:** 18 Years
*   **Maximum Age:** 33 Years
*   Age Relaxation Extra as per RRB NTPC 10+2 Level CEN 07/2026 Recruitment Rules.

### How to Apply for RRB NTPC 10+2 Level Online Form 2026
1. Ensure you have all necessary documents ready (ID Proof, Address Details, Basic Details).
2. Scan required documents related to the recruitment form (Photo, Sign, ID Proof, etc.).
3. Visit the official RRB Apply portal.
4. If you are a new user, complete the One Time Registration (OTR).
5. Log in, select CEN 07/2026, and fill out the application form carefully.
6. Pay the application fee and submit the final form. 
7. Take a printout of the submitted form.
