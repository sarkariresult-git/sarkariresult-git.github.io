# Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC)
## UPSSSC Stenographer Recruitment 2025 Result
### Short Details of Notification

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has declared the written exam eligibility result for the Stenographer Recruitment 2025. Candidates who enrolled for this vacancy can now download their results and check their eligibility for the upcoming Skill Test/Typing Test. 

### Important Dates
*   **Application Begin:** October 2025
*   **Last Date for Apply Online:** November 2025
*   **Exam Date:** August 2026
*   **Result Available:** 30/10/2026
*   **Skill Test Date:** To be notified soon

### How to Check UPSSSC Stenographer Result 2026
1. Visit the official website of UPSSSC.
2. Click on the "Results" section on the homepage.
3. Select the link for "UPSSSC Stenographer Eligibility Result 2025".
4. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and Captcha Code.
5. Click on "See Result" to view your qualifying status for the skill test.
6. Take a printout of the result for future reference.
