# Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC)
## Bihar School Teacher Recruitment Exam TRE 4.0 2026
### Short Details of Notification

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has officially announced the Teacher Recruitment Exam (TRE) 4.0 for various teaching posts, including Primary (Class 1-5), Middle (Class 6-8), Secondary (Class 9-10), and Higher Secondary (Class 11-12). Eligible and interested candidates can read the full notification and apply online through the official BPSC portal.

### Important Dates
*   **Application Begin:** 28/10/2026
*   **Last Date for Apply Online:** 18/11/2026
*   **Pay Exam Fee Last Date:** 18/11/2026
*   **Correction Date:** Notified Soon
*   **Exam Date:** To be Notified
*   **Admit Card Available:** Before Exam

### Application Fee
*   **General / OBC / Other State:** ₹ 750/-
*   **SC / ST / PH:** ₹ 200/-
*   **Female Candidate (Bihar Dom.):** ₹ 200/-
*   **Note:** Candidates need to pay an additional biometric fee of ₹ 200/- per post if they do not provide their Aadhaar Number during the application. Pay the examination fee through Debit Card, Credit Card, or Net Banking.

### Age Limit as on 01/08/2026
*   **Minimum Age:** 18 Years for Primary Teachers.
*   **Minimum Age:** 21 Years for Middle / Secondary / Higher Secondary Teachers.
*   **Maximum Age:** 37 Years (Male) & 40 Years (Female).
*   Age Relaxation Extra as per BPSC School Teacher TRE 4.0 Recruitment Rules.

### Vacancy Details 
**Total Posts:** *33,320 Positions* *Refer to official notification for exact post-wise distribution.*

| Post Name | Eligibility Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Teacher (Class 1-5)** | Senior Secondary (10+2) with 50% Marks and 2 Year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed) <br> **OR** Bachelor's Degree in any stream with D.El.Ed. <br> CTET Paper 1 or BTET Paper 1 Passed. |
| **Middle Teacher (Class 6-8)** | Bachelor's Degree with 2 Year D.El.Ed **OR** Bachelor's/Master's Degree with 50% Marks and B.Ed. <br> CTET Paper 2 or BTET Paper 2 Passed. |
| **Secondary Teacher (Class 9-10)** | Bachelor/Master Degree in Related Subject with minimum 50% Marks and B.Ed Degree. <br> STET Paper 1 Passed. |
| **Higher Secondary (Class 11-12)** | Master Degree in Related Subject with Minimum 50% Marks and B.Ed Degree. <br> STET Paper 2 Passed. |

### How to Apply for BPSC TRE 4.0 Online Form 2026
1. Visit the BPSC online application portal.
2. Read the TRE 4.0 official notification carefully before starting the registration.
3. Keep all required documents ready: Aadhaar Card, Educational Certificates, CTET/STET Marksheets, Photo, and Signature.
4. Fill in your basic details to register and generate a Username and Password.
5. Log in to your account and fill out the detailed application form.
6. Upload scanned images of your photo, signature, and necessary documents.
7. Pay the application fee and submit the final form.
8. Download and print the submitted application form for future reference.
