# Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
## SSC Junior Engineer (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical) Exam 2026
### Short Details of Notification

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the Admit Card and Application Status for the Junior Engineer (JE) Paper 1 Examination 2026. Candidates who have successfully applied for the SSC JE 2026 exam can now download their hall tickets from their respective regional SSC websites.

### Important Dates
*   **Application Begin:** July 2026
*   **Last Date for Apply Online:** August 2026
*   **Paper I Exam Date:** 15-18 November 2026
*   **Application Status Available:** 25/10/2026
*   **Admit Card Available:** 30/10/2026

### SSC JE 2026 Vacancy Details
*   **Posts Available:** Junior Engineer (Civil), Junior Engineer (Electrical), Junior Engineer (Mechanical).
*   **Departments:** CPWD, BRO, CWC, Farakka Barrage, MES, NTRO, Ministry of Ports, etc.

### How to Download SSC JE Admit Card 2026
1. Scroll down to the "Important Links" section below or visit the SSC Regional Website you applied from (e.g., CR, NR, MPR, WR).
2. Click on the "Download SSC JE Admit Card 2026" link.
3. Enter your Registration ID / Roll Number / Name along with your Date of Birth.
4. Solve the captcha verification and click "Search" or "Submit".
5. Your exam date, shift time, and city details will appear.
6. The full Admit Card with the exact center address will be available to download 4 days prior to your specific exam date.
7. Print the admit card on A4 size paper.
